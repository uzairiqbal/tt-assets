TShirts & Trousers Studio Bot

Personal automation tool

A private automation tool that generates professional product studio shots and video reels from garment photos, then publishes them to social media platforms.

  • Sends a garment photo via Telegram
  • Generates 3 studio-quality product shots (flat-lay, ghost-mannequin, folded)
  • Builds a short product reel
  • Publishes to Instagram and TikTok after owner approval

This tool is operated solely for the @tshirtsandtrousers_ social media accounts. It is not a public service and does not process data from any third party.
