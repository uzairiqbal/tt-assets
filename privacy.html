Privacy Policy

TShirts & Trousers Studio Bot  ·  Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how TShirts & Trousers Studio Bot ("the Bot", "we", "us") handles information. The Bot is a personal automation tool operated solely by its individual owner for managing a single social media account (@tshirtsandtrousers_). It is not a public service.

2. What data the Bot accesses

The Bot accesses only the owner's own accounts and content:

  • TikTok account: The Bot uses OAuth to authenticate as the account owner. It requests the video.upload and video.publish scopes to post product videos on the owner's behalf. No data from TikTok users other than the account owner is accessed.
  • Video content: Product garment photos and generated video reels created by the owner. This content is uploaded to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

3. What data the Bot does NOT collect

  • Personal data of any third party
  • Viewer or follower data
  • Analytics or engagement metrics
  • Any information beyond what is needed to post content to the owner's account

4. Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the Bot's private GitHub Actions repository and are never shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party beyond the platforms they authenticate against (TikTok, Instagram).

5. Third-party platforms

When the Bot publishes content to TikTok, TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply to that content and the associated account. This Bot does not control how TikTok processes data after a post is published.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated at any time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
